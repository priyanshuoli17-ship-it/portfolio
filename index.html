01 IoT & Embedded

Smart IoT-Based Hydration Monitoring Bottle

Developed an intelligent IoT-enabled smart bottle to monitor water volume in real time using HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors and Arduino Uno.

  • Real-time percentage calculation and live status rendering on a 16×2 I2C LCD.
  • Automated buzzer and LED alert system when water drops below threshold.
  • Integrated PIR sensor for human presence detection and low-power standby mode.
  • ESP8266 Wi-Fi connectivity syncing hydration analytics to ThingSpeak Cloud.
